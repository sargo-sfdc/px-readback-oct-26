How we’d implement

  • Phase 1: Move core CRM from HubSpot (advertiser and publisher pipelines, contacts, companies, deals and tickets), and connect ClickHouse through Data Cloud so net revenue is on every account and deal from day one
  • Phase 2: Proactive insights and workflows in Slack and Claude, built on that unified data
  • Phase 3: Expand into marketing automation and ad optimization
  • Partner-led, and sized for a lean RevOps team
